What is the Ego Audit?

The Ego Audit is an analysis of your ego in a one-on-one video call with me.

Who this is for

This audit is for people who have had an intense experience or realization that their logical mind cannot resolve:

  • Nihilistic Overthinking: You crossed a line in your own thinking that you cannot uncross. Now meaning feels arbitrary but the searching won’t stop.
  • Ontological Shock: You experienced an altered state of consciousness that shattered what you thought was possible. Now you are struggling to connect to “normal” reality.
  • Existential Unrest: You’ve seen there’s much more to reality than what society believes, but now you can’t un-see what you’ve seen. Instead of finding freedom, you became more aware of your prison.
  • Identity Collapse: The loss of someone has broken your internal model of fairness, control, or predictability, leaving you wondering who you are outside of that relationship.
  • Workaholic Burnout: You achieved success but the promised satisfaction never came. Realizing your drive was built on a false narrative, you are left empty.

Not for: Those seeking emotional validation, strategic life coaching, or anyone unwilling to examine their beliefs under pressure.

Proceed only if knowing matters more to you than being comforted.

Why audit the ego?

The ego is the system that runs your sense of self. It is made out of your thought patterns, emotions, behavioral loops and the personal stories that hold it all together. Left unchecked, it creates endless existential searching, chronic disconnection and the persistent sense that something is wrong.

The goal is clarity: to see your identity and its contradictions for what they are, named precisely.

Ego Architecture: The 6-Phase Protocol

The audit follows a six-phase structure designed to move from surface narratives to underlying ego mechanics:

Phase 01: Establishing Ground

We establish a space where social performance serves no function. This phase clarifies boundaries, vulnerabilities, and your specific objectives for the session. We determine what you want from this assessment and agree on how deep we’ll go.

Phase 02: Narrative Architecture Mapping

We identify the stories you tell about who you are and how you position yourself relative to past events. These narratives function as load-bearing structures of your internal architecture.

Phase 03: Core Driver Isolation

Through direct questioning about values, desires, and behavioral patterns, we identify what moves you forward versus what you believe should move you forward. We look beneath surface motivations to find the need or fear actually driving your mind.

Phase 04: Contradiction Stress Test

I challenge your self-concept by identifying contradictions between your stated beliefs and observable reality. I apply pressure to see which parts of your identity model hold integrity and which collapse under scrutiny. Beyond truth, this is about the relationship to your own certainty.

Phase 05: Structured Silence

I guide you into deliberate silence following the challenge phase. We observe what emerges when your mind cannot intellectually justify itself. What arises in this space... or what rushes to fill it?

Phase 06: The Ego Architecture Verdict

I deliver my unfiltered structural analysis: how your ego operates, where the contradictions exist, and the source of your internal conflicts. I answer what questions remain, and you receive the verdict in writing afterwards.
