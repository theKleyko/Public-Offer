3. Visitor statistics (Plausible), no cookies

This site uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. To understand how the pages are used, it uses Plausible Analytics by Plausible Insights OÜ (Estonia, EU), which processes data in the European Union and stores no IP addresses or user identifiers. Counted in aggregate: page views, where visits come from (for example YouTube or Substack), approximate country, device type, and some actions on the page: pressing the booking button or a contact link, playing a testimonial video, reaching the end of the page or staying on it for a while. This site's short links (for example /substack) count the click before taking you to their destination. If your browser sends a do-not-track signal (Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track), nothing is counted. Legal basis: legitimate interest in knowing, statistically, how the site is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). More information: Plausible's data policy.
